## What the user asked for

1. Add **army support** and **citizen support** as indicators, like stability and war support.
2. Replace the Economy / Military / Politics / Diplomacy / Journal display with a **small rectangular panel** showing the key indicators **only for the player's nation**. When another country is clicked, show only **limited information** about it.
3. Add a **drop-down overlay** over the story/order area with detailed information about the player's country. It must include a leader **portrait** from `public/img/country_leaders_portraits` (matched to the right country), and portraits of different ratios must not break the window.
4. Add **advisors** (economic advisor, diplomat, military advisor). They write short notes every turn with historical context, about the situation inside and outside the country, and open from a drop-down in the bottom panel.

## What was done

- **Indicators**: `army_support` and `citizen_support` were added to both campaigns with a starting value for every nation. Older saves are migrated on load (`migrateState` in `server/engine.js`), and the AI agents are told what the indicators mean.
- **Compact panel**: the header shows your flag, name, leader and war status, with a **Details** button. The five tabs show stat tiles, a diplomacy summary (faction, wars, best and worst relations) or a short journal. The body has a fixed height.
- **Intel card**: clicking another nation on the map or in the Diplomacy tab opens a small card. It shows the portrait (if any), leader, faction, relations with you and a word-based "intelligence estimate", with no exact numbers.
- **Country report**: **Details** drops a report down over the story log. It has:
  - the leader's portrait in a fixed 3:4 frame
  - the key facts
  - every indicator with a bar, the change last turn, the change since the start, and a help text
  - provinces held, enemy forces inside your borders and your forces abroad
  - relations with every major power
- **Portraits**: `public/js/portraits.js` matches by the current leader's name: abe, antonescu, boris, carol, chamberlain, churchill, gaulle, hitler, kai-shek, lebrun, mao, moscicki, mussolini, roosevelt, stalin, tito. France under Daladier shows Lebrun, labelled as head of state.
- **Advisors**: a new agent runs after the rival leaders every turn and returns an outlook plus home / abroad / suggestion for each advisor.
  - The opening briefing for each playable nation is hand-written, in `server/data/advisors/`.
  - Offline, later briefings are generated from the game data.
  - The UI is an **Advisors** bar above the order box. It has three advisor buttons with outlook dots and a "new" badge. It opens a drop-up with a tab per advisor and a **Use as order** button.
- **Tests and docs**: new `tests/advisors.test.js`, including an LLM-path test against a fake local server. README and docs were updated, and a change report was added.
- **Also recorded**: `change-reports/2026-09-19-miniature-flags.md` covers the earlier flag work, which had no report.
